## Preserved gameplay claims
The article records an end-to-end Cold Plum advanced-swordsman quest:
1. 古大叔（Goo）offers work and directs the player toward 楊子陵.
2. 楊子陵（Yang zlin）describes the missing medicine and snake-gall route.
3. A hidden Snow Mountain cave contains 千年白蟒; corpse yields 蛇膽.
4. 歐陽鐘（Ou yang zhong）accepts the gall and 50 gold and supplies medicine.
5. 梅夫人 accepts 九轉冰心丹 and gives 寒梅玉珮.
6. 寒梅玉珮 is recorded with 冷梅劍法 +10, 傷害力 +5, 傲梅暗劍訣 +10.
7. 梅影風（May yin fong）accepts the jade, demonstrates Cold Plum sword essence, and the record shows 膽識 +2.
